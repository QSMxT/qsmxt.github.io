The QSMxT Ecosystem

Turn MRI phase into susceptibility maps: reconstruct in your browser with QSMbly, process a whole study from the command line with QSMxT, or get a map back at the scanner console with QSMxT Recon. They all run QSM.rs, a Rust library with 40+ algorithms, and QSM-CI shows you how those methods and others actually perform. All free and open source.

QSM.rs
Library

Every stage of the reconstruction, from brain extraction to dipole inversion, in one Rust crate. Everything else here runs this code.
